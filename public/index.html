    SUPER EASY GUIDE

    Pick a game. Pick a die. Have fun.

    👀
    1. Wait for your name

    When the game says “YOUR TURN,” it is your turn.

    🎲
    2. Pick one of the two dice

    Normal Die is safer. Deadly Risk Die can give 10, 20, or 30, but skulls hurt.

    💰
    3. Tap “Bank points”

    This saves the pot into your big score. Saved points are yours.

    ☝️
    4. Be careful with 1

    Roll a 1 and the pot goes away. You also lose 5 saved points.

    ⏰
    5. Tap before time runs out

    Use the timer on screen. If it reaches zero, your turn ends.

    🏆
    6. Win your game type

    In Game 1, reach the target or win Showdown. In Game 2, knock out your friends and be the last one standing.

    Show me the extra tricks
    • Safety Net: Your first bad roll does not take 5 points.
    • Hot Streak: Three good rolls in one turn give you 10 extra points.
    • Risk Die: Five faces pay 10, 10, 20, 20, or 30. Five skulls bust you, and more skulls appear as your streak grows.
    • Battle Dice: Everyone starts with 30 health. Normal rolls hit the next rival for 2–6, while a 1 hurts you for 5. The Deadly Risk Die hits for 10, 20, or 30, but a skull hurts you for 10. Last player standing wins.
    • Freeze: Spend 5 saved points to make a friend miss one turn. Use it once each turn.
